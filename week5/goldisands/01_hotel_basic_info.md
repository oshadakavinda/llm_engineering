# Goldi Sands Hotel - Basic Information

## Hotel Overview
Goldi Sands Hotel is a glamorous iconic beach hotel nestled on the golden beaches of Negombo, Sri Lanka. The hotel is located right on the beach next to the Indian Ocean, offering guests direct beach access at any time of day or night.

## Location Details
- **Address**: Ethukala, Negombo, Sri Lanka
- **Distance from Airport**: 15 minutes from Bandaranaike International Airport
- **Distance from Colombo**: 32 kilometers from Colombo city
- **Beach Location**: Located directly on Negombo Beach with Indian Ocean views
