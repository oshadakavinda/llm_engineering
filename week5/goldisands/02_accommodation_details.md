# Goldi Sands Hotel - Accommodation Details

## Room Inventory
The hotel features **131 air-conditioned rooms** in total:
- 5 Junior Suites
- 2 Duplex Suites  
- 57 Deluxe Sea View Rooms
- 57 Standard Sea View Rooms
- 10 Economy Rooms

## Room Capacity
- **Economy Rooms**: Accommodate 2 guests
- **All Other Rooms**: Accommodate 2 adults plus 2 children
- Most rooms feature private balconies or terraces
- Excellent views of Indian Ocean and landscaped gardens

## Standard Room Amenities
All rooms include:
- Air conditioning
- King-size or twin beds
- Work desk
- Complimentary Wi-Fi
- International dial telephone
- Smart TV
- Electronic safety box
- Mini-refrigerator/Mini-bar
- Tea and coffee making facilities
- Sitting area
- En-suite bathroom with rain shower
- Hot and cold water
- Hair dryer
- Piped music system

## Premium Room Features (Deluxe Wing)
Additional amenities in deluxe accommodations:
- Bathrobes and slippers
- Enhanced bathroom facilities
- Connecting room options available
- Premium furnishing and decor
